Software Engineer Intern

Sunquick Lanka Pvt Ltd | 2025 Dec - 2026 June

During my internship at Sunquick Lanka Pvt Ltd, I gained hands-on experience in full-stack software development by contributing to real-world industry applications and internal business systems. I was involved in developing and maintaining web applications using modern technologies such as CodeIgniter (CI4), React, PHP, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and SQL.

I worked on industry-level internal websites and portals that supported business operations, including service management and dispenser tracking systems. My responsibilities included developing responsive user interfaces, handling backend logic, integrating APIs, managing databases, and improving system workflows. I also worked with REST APIs for data communication between systems and applications.

In addition to web development, I gained exposure to mobile application development using Android Studio, where I contributed to features related to dispenser tracking and QR-based operations. I also developed a basic understanding of Microsoft Dynamics 365 (D365) and its role in manufacturing and operational workflows.
